# Feature Specification — MP News Portal

## 1. Product Overview
A modern Hindi digital-news platform focused primarily on Madhya Pradesh, with national and international coverage. The platform is inspired by the broad content organization and media experience of major Indian news portals such as Dainik Bhaskar, while using an original brand, UI, content, and implementation.
